# Product

## Platform

ios

## Users

- **Walker (primary):** a college student walking home alone at night on campus (demo: ASU Tempe), phone in hand, glancing at it while moving. Job: get home, answer the buddy's occasional check-ins with a pet, and know friends are watching without having to think about it.
- **Friend (circle member):** opens the app mostly when something happens. Job: see that a friend is walking, and when an alert fires, understand at once what happened, where, and what to do, then close the alert.

## Product Purpose

Pupdate makes the walk home safer by turning check-ins into caring for a pet. The buddy (Melvin, a dog by default) checks in when something looks off; the walker answers by petting it. If they stop answering, it escalates (check-in, nudge, last chance, countdown) and then the walker's circle gets a full-screen alert with the last location. Success: the walker keeps the app running every walk because it's pleasant, and a friend reacts within seconds when it matters.

## Positioning

A safety app that feels like raising a pet. The check-in is a rub on the buddy, not a "Are you safe? Yes/No" dialog, and the reward loop (XP, streak, evolution) is what makes people open it every night. The safety engine is deterministic and never waits on AI; Gemini only writes the buddy's lines, safer-route notes and friend briefs.

## Operating Context

- Used outdoors at night, often one-handed while walking, sometimes with the screen locked (Lock Screen Live Activity and Dynamic Island carry the walk state and a Pet button).
- Walk flow: Home -> Plan (Apple Maps search, safer-route pick, flags) -> Walk screen (next turn, buddy, escalation ladder, live map, pace) -> end walk -> Past walks.
- Friend flow: feed of walking friends, live map, alert takeover with Gemini brief, close alert.
- Shared flagged spots (Avoid / Caution / OK) on maps.
- Hackathon demo: one or two phones, sim walker controls, roles picked on first screen ("I'm walking home (Jordan)" / "I'm the friend (Maya)").

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Native SwiftUI iPhone app + WidgetKit Live Activity. iOS 17+ APIs (MapKit for SwiftUI).
- Duress (2 s long-press on the buddy) must stay invisible on the walker's screen, Live Activity and logs.
- Only a friend closes an alert.
- No push notifications: friend alerts need the app open.
- UI conventions in CLAUDE.md are binding (one fullScreenCover presenter per screen via ScreenPanel, publish-only-on-change stores, in-place overlays, Typeface/Palette/Motion tokens, accessibility identifiers used by UI tests).
- Everything says Pupdate; only bundle IDs, the server folder/unit, the MongoDB database and `wb.*` settings keys keep the old `walkbuddy` name.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Pupdate**. Tagline in the app: "Your pup walks you home and keeps your friends updated."
- The generated dog art (DogView / DogArt.swift from swift/tools/dog_art.py), its moods and its forms (Puppy -> Scout Pup -> Guardian Dog) stay as they are.
- The pet / Tamagotchi game feel is binding: XP, streaks, evolution, the buddy as the centre of the experience.
- Voice: warm, plain, a little playful from the buddy; calm and direct in alerts ("Do not call first").

## Product Principles

1. The buddy is the interface: safety interactions happen through the pet, not through forms.
2. Glanceable while walking: the one thing that matters now is readable in a second, at arm's length, at night.
3. Calm until it isn't: quiet in the normal case; escalation is unmistakable and the friend's alert leaves no doubt about what to do.
4. Safety never depends on AI or on the app looking nice: fallbacks first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Binding: tap-to-pet as an alternative to rubbing, larger text (in-app scale and iPhone Dynamic Type), reduce motion, spoken prompts, sounds, strong alert vibration, VoiceOver labels on every control.
